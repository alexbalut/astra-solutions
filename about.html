About

Built in Montréal for teams that care about the details

Astra Solutions is a B2B software practice: custom tools, integrations, and products like GymQR—grounded in how businesses actually run day to day.

We started from a simple observation: a lot of software sold to small and mid-sized businesses is either too generic or overly complex. Gyms, service desks, and back offices need tools that fit the work.

GymQR Guide grew from conversations with gym operators in Montréal who needed better machine-side guidance and a lighter way for members to track workouts. It’s our flagship product and an example of how we design: practical, editable by staff, and easy to use.

Beyond GymQR, we take on custom software and integration work for teams who need something their current stack can’t do. Same standards either way—clear scope, usable delivery, honest communication.

We’re rooted in Montréal and Québec. English is fine for most clients; we’re comfortable working with operators across the city and beyond.

Working name. “Astra Solutions” is our working company name while we grow the practice and products. The work and the contact channels are real; branding may evolve.
