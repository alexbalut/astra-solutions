Privacy note

A short, honest summary for this static marketing site.

This website is a static marketing site for Astra Solutions. It does not run a backend contact database and does not intentionally collect personal information through the contact form.

Contact form

The contact form validates your input in the browser and shows a success message. Form data is not sent to or stored on our servers from this demo deployment. If you email us (for example at hello@astrasolutions.ca), that correspondence is handled like normal email.

Hosting & logs

If this site is hosted on GitHub Pages or a similar static host, the host may collect standard server or CDN logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested). We do not control those systems beyond choosing a host.

Fonts

Pages may load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. Google may process connection data according to their own policies when fonts are fetched.

Cookies

This site does not set analytics or advertising cookies. Your browser may still store ordinary technical data for fonts or caching.

Products such as GymQR

If you use GymQR or other Astra software products later, those products will have their own privacy terms covering member and operator data. This page only covers the marketing website.
