Solutions · Flagship product

GymQR Guide

QR codes on machines. Clear instructions for members. Simple workout logging. Content you control—built for gym floors, starting in Montréal.

The problem

New members hesitate at machines. Staff repeat the same cues. Printed posters go stale. And most “fitness apps” are built for consumers—not for operators who need branded, editable guides at the equipment itself.

GymQR puts the instruction where the work happens: scan → understand → train. Operators update content without waiting on a redesign.

What GymQR includes

What it covers—kept practical.

Machine QR guides

Each piece of equipment gets a code that opens a clear, mobile-friendly instruction page—setup, form cues, safety notes.

Workout tracking

Members can log sets without navigating a heavyweight consumer app. Keep the flow short enough for a busy floor.

Operator-controlled content

Update guides, branding, and machine lists yourself. No waiting on a designer every time you rearrange the floor.

How we work with gyms

A practical rollout path—no fabricated case studies, just the process.

  1. Understand the space

    We inventory machines, traffic patterns, and what staff already explain by hand.

  2. Set up content

    Guides, QR placement, and branding aligned to your gym—not a generic template dump.

  3. Soft launch

    Pilot with a subset of equipment or members; adjust based on real use.

  4. Own & iterate

    You control content going forward. We stay available for support and expansions.

For gym owners & managers

Reduce repeated floor questions, give new members confidence, and keep instructions current when equipment changes.
