Services

What we build and how we deliver

Four lanes of work—custom builds, integrations, productized tools, and support—so you can start with what you need, not a package deal.

01

Custom software

When spreadsheets, generic SaaS, or a patchwork of tools stop scaling with your operation, we design and build software around how you actually work.

Typical scope includes internal dashboards, member- or client-facing web apps, staff workflows, and lightweight admin tools. We keep the stack pragmatic and the interface clear.

  • Discovery workshops with operators and staff
  • Web apps prioritized for real devices on the floor or in the office
  • Handoff documentation and training for your team

02

Integrations & APIs

Most businesses already have a stack. We connect the pieces—billing, CRM, access systems, messaging, spreadsheets—so data moves without manual copy-paste.

We prefer durable, documented integrations over fragile one-offs. If an API doesn’t exist yet, we help define one that your future tools can reuse.

  • API design and connector development
  • Webhook and sync flows between existing tools
  • Clear ownership of credentials, environments, and failure modes

03

Productized tools

Some problems repeat across operators. For those, we build products you can adopt faster—configured to your brand, without starting from a blank repo.

GymQR Guide is our flagship example: QR-based machine instructions and workout tracking aimed at gym floors. It’s one product in a broader services practice—not the only thing we do.

  • Faster path from decision to live on the floor
  • Content and branding you control
  • Room to customize when your operation needs it

Learn about GymQR →

04

Implementation & support

Software only helps if people use it. We stay through rollout: setup, staff training, content migration, and a practical support channel after launch.

  • Onboarding plans sized to your team
  • Content and QR setup guidance for products like GymQR
  • Ongoing fixes and small improvements on agreed terms

Not sure which lane fits?

Describe the problem. We’ll tell you honestly whether custom, productized, or “you don’t need us yet” is the right answer.
